What the FACO training tools are built on, and who owns what.

Aeronautical data

The Flight Instruments tool draws its airports, runways, ILS and localizer approaches, VORs, VORTACs, TACANs, DMEs and NDBs from the Federal Aviation Administration's National Airspace System Resource (NASR) data. FAA data is a work of the United States Government and is in the public domain. It was prepared for the tool using the open-source @squawk data packages. The NASR cycle in use is shown in the tool's help window.

The data has no terrain and is not kept up to date for you. It is for training only and must not be used for navigation.

Phaser

Flight Instruments and Briefing Lines draw their maps and instruments with Phaser 4, an open-source HTML5 game framework, distributed here as brief/phaser.min.js under the MIT License.
